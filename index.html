🏥 NovaCare EHR
AI-Powered Bed Placement System — Project Overview
😩 The Problem
🧠
Mental overload every single admission
When a new patient arrives, a nurse has to mentally check 20 beds, remember which nurses are full, know which beds need isolation, and pick the right one — all while the patient is waiting. They do this 30–50 times a shift.
📝
20–30 minutes of handoff writing every shift change
At the end of every shift, the outgoing nurse manually writes a summary of every patient for the incoming nurse. It is repetitive, stressful, and happens 3 times every 24 hours.
🚨
Critical beds go empty while patients wait
A stable P1 patient who could safely move to a regular ward stays in the ICU because nobody has time to review every patient for step-down eligibility. Meanwhile a new critical patient waits in the queue.
✅ What NovaCare Does
🤖
AI picks the bed, not the nurse
When a patient arrives, the AI checks all 20 beds in under a second and picks the best one. It looks at the ward, the nurse workload, the complaint, and whether isolation is needed. The nurse sees the reason and confirms in one click.
🧠
Suggests the priority before the nurse decides
The AI reads the vitals — blood pressure, heart rate, SpO₂, temperature, pain score — and suggests whether the patient is P1 Critical, P2 Urgent, P3 Less Urgent, or P4 Non-Urgent. The nurse can accept or change it. The AI never forces a decision.
📋
Writes the handoff report automatically
At the end of every shift, one click generates a full handoff — every patient, every pending action, every nurse assignment. What used to take 20 to 30 minutes of manual writing now takes 30 seconds.
⬇
Frees critical beds without waiting for discharge
When the ICU is nearly full, the AI finds stable P1 patients who can safely move to a regular ward and suggests the move to the charge nurse. A critical bed opens up without anyone having to manually review every chart.
🚑
Holds a bed for an incoming ambulance
When paramedics call ahead, the charge nurse reserves a specific bed before the patient arrives. The AI skips that bed in all other assignments until the patient is admitted.
